A SHORT INTRODUCTION

About

I am interested in understanding software beyond its surface — how it is built, how data moves through it, and how unexpected behavior emerges from implementation details.

Areas of interest

  • Reverse engineering and binary analysis
  • Malware analysis and defensive research
  • Software security and vulnerability research
  • Systems programming, debugging, and tooling

Why this blog?

This blog is a public learning log. I use it to document methods, preserve useful findings, and explain technical ideas in a way that my future self can understand.

Opinions and experiments here are my own. Research posts should include clear scope, reproducible context where appropriate, and responsible handling of security findings.
